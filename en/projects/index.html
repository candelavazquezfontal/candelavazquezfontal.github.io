PRJ-001

Queueing and Simulation: Zara mega-stores

Optimization and Simulation · Deusto · Feb – May 2026 · group project (3 people)
+
Course with Matrícula de Honor

Problem

Course project on the online-order pickup and packing process of Zara mega-stores.

What was done

Modelled the process as a multi-server queue, comparing a single mega-store with five distributed stores, and simulated a full year of operation (777,780 orders).

My part

Group project, 3 people.

Results

  • System saturated in the 16:00–21:00 slot in both scenarios and unable to recover its backlog (−795 and −390 orders/day).
  • One-year simulation: 62.58% of orders served and 290,030 lost sales, all in the overnight slot.
  • Proposal: notify customers of delays, cap the online order queue and add afternoon and night capacity.

Queueing theorySimulation

PRJ-002

EcoPlay: circular toy subscription

Product Engineering · Deusto · Sep 2025 – Jan 2026 · group project (3 people)
+
Course with Matrícula de Honor

Problem

Business model for a subscription service of refurbished toys.

What was done

Business Model Canvas and Value Proposition Canvas, economic model, and a validation test with 7 critical hypotheses and 4 customer interviews.

My part

Group project, 3 people.

Results

  • Break-even at 411 active monthly subscribers and a €25,000 net profit target in year 3.
  • €13 variable cost per customer and plans at €19.90, €25.90 and €34.90 a month.
  • Validation: 80% understood the concept and asked about delivery times, with no reason found to pivot.
